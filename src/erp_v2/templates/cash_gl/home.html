Cash GL re-point

Finance

Each bank account gets its own cash GL account. A re-point is a proposal; a different person must approve it. It is refused once the bank account has an approved reconciliation, while payments for it are unposted, or when the target is already another bank account's cash GL. Re-pointing moves no balance: Finance reclassifies with B2 journals (see the runbook).
